## ⚖️ Storyteller rules (the only three)

1. **School-appropriate.** Your principal is a possible player.
2. **Funny beats gory.** "Detention... FOREVER" is scarier than anything with slime in it.
3. **Dead ends need a reason.** Every arrow must point at a real room or ending —
   an arrow to nowhere is a bug, and we fix bugs on paper, where it's cheap.

## 🧠 Brain teaser (optional — take it home)

**Design a SECRET BONUS ROOM.** A room the map doesn't show: how do players find it
(a hidden third choice? a password?), what's inside, and where does it lead? Same
sheet format, same three rules. **The best secret room gets built into the game next
week** — with the designers' names in the credits.
