## 🧠 The final brain teaser (take it home — you've earned it)

**Week 1, you programmed a human to make a sandwich. Now close the circle:**
teach someone at home to program *YOU* making a sandwich. They give the
instructions; you follow them *exactly* — literally, mercilessly, like the machine
you now understand. When it goes hilariously wrong, teach them what you'd teach a
computer: *be precise, go in order, and when something breaks — read the error,
fix one step, run it again.*
